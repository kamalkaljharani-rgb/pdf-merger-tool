    How to Merge PDF Files in 3 Simple Steps

    Combining your PDF documents takes only a few seconds. Follow these quick steps to merge your files:

    1. 1.
      Select Your Files

      Drag and drop your PDF files into the upload box above, or click "Browse Files" to choose them from your device. You can add reports, invoices, tax forms, scanned receipts, contracts, or presentations.

    2. 2.
      Arrange the Page Order

      Your combined document will follow the order of your list from top to bottom.

      • ○ Use the Up ( ^ ) and Down ( ∨ ) buttons next to any file to change its position.
      • ○ Put your cover sheet at the top, followed by your main content and appendices.
      • ○ Click the Remove ( ✕ ) button to delete a file added by mistake, or click "Clear All" to start over.
    3. 3.
      Merge & Download PDF

      Click Merge & Download PDF. A live progress bar will show you the status in real time. Once finished, your merged document will automatically download directly to your computer.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this PDF merger really free? +

    Yes, 100% free with zero hidden costs. You can combine unlimited PDF files without creating an account, installing software, or paying for a subscription. Because our engine processes your documents directly on your computer, we don't have expensive cloud server costs to pass on to you—allowing us to keep the tool completely free to use.

    Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere? +

    No. Your files never leave your computer. Unlike traditional tools that send your documents to remote cloud servers over the internet, our app combines your PDFs entirely inside your web browser. Your confidential information, private data, and document contents remain 100% on your local device at all times.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once? +

    You can combine as many files as you need. Our tool is designed to handle large batches smoothly right inside your web browser. Processing capacity depends on your device's memory. If you are merging dozens of very large or image-heavy scanned documents and encounter an issue, try splitting them into smaller groups first and then combining the final files.

    Can I change the order before downloading? +

    Yes, absolutely. Your final PDF will follow the exact top-to-bottom order of your list. You can easily adjust the sequence using the Up ( ^ ) and Down ( ∨ ) buttons next to any file before clicking Merge & Download PDF.

    Why did my PDF fail to merge? +

    Some PDFs have special security settings or file corruption. If a file fails to process, it is usually because it is password-protected, encrypted, or corrupted. To fix this, simply click Remove ( ✕ ) next to the problem file and try merging again. Alternatively, open the document in your PDF reader, print or save it as a new PDF, and upload the new version.
